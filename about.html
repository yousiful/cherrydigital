Who We Are

Family-run, locally-owned — serving Cincinnati since 1994

Welcome to Cherry Digital Pros, the best firm for computer repair services and IT services. Since 1994, we have been providing all computer-related services to the Greater Cincinnati Metropolitan area. We provide professional, quality workmanship with every computer service we provide.

We offer a range of services such as computer repair services, networking, data recovery, laptop repair, and computer maintenance to both businesses and individuals. From start to finish we'll keep you posted on the progress and estimated duration of the repair for all computer repair services.

If you're looking for a computer tune-up to speed up your current system or fix a system that just won't stay up, we've got you covered. We offer both in-store and remote services to make things easy for you. Our expert computer technicians are here to solve your problems and get you back to enjoying your computer ASAP.

We are a family-run, locally-owned business. We are dedicated to being a responsible corporate citizen. We strive to make the lives of our customers and neighbors better every day. We believe that the success of our company is dependent on the health of our communities.

We shall make every effort to gain your respect and trust. We provide the greatest service for the cheapest price. We will provide you with honest guidance and a fair fee for our products. We think there are no quick cuts to producing high-quality work. We won't compromise quality for expediency.
