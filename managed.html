What Are Managed Services?

Managed Services is a business and consumer model where a third-party company called a Managed Services Provider (MSP / RMM) takes on the day-to-day monitoring of the devices connected to our managed service servers.

We monitor hardware changes, software changes and updates, Virus and Ransomware threats. We have a 97% detection policy for your devices — nothing is 100%, but we come close!

Managed Service Provider software is a suite of tools for IT providers to remotely manage client IT environments, focusing on automation, monitoring, security, and efficiency. It combines functions like Remote Monitoring & Management (RMM), Professional Services Automation (PSA), ticketing, billing, and patching into unified platforms to streamline operations for multiple clients.
